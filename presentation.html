● The Problem & Scientific Urgency

The Peril of Vanishing Space Heritage

Degradation, Dispersion, and the Need for a Digital Twin

1. Physical Degradation in Deep Space

Over 70 humanity-crafted landers, rovers, and probes reside across celestial bodies. Without protective atmospheres on the Moon or under micrometeorite bombardment and dust storms on Mars, physical sites face micro-erosion, solar UV degradation, and thermal stress.

2. Fragmented Historical Provenance

Coordinates, telemetry, mission reports, and scientific claims are scattered across disparate NASA PDS volumes, Soviet archival releases, and academic papers with conflicting coordinates.

The AFTERLIGHT Imperative: Deliver an authoritative, mathematically verified 3D digital twin of humanity's lunar and Martian heritage with exact coordinates, authentic telemetry, and multi-chapter cinematic storytelling.

Strict Chapter-Based Audio Routing

  • Absolute Silence on Open/Idle: Opening or browsing produces zero audio clutter.
  • CH 1 to 4: rocket_space_travel_12s.wav (soft deep space cruise).
  • CH 5: landing_increasing_thruster_ch5.wav (Part 1: 12s descent burn).
  • CH 6: landing_increasing_thruster_ch6.wav (Part 2: 12s terminal cushion to reduce impact).

Instant Pause & Zero Race Conditions

Zero-Latency Audio Cutoff: Clicking Pause, pressing Spacebar, or scrubbing immediately drops gain to 0 and disconnects all nodes.
  • Generation-Token Gating: Prevents async buffer fetches from playing after pause.
  • Timeline Alignment: Resumes at exact millisecond offset on scrubber.
  • Procedural Fallback: 48 kHz Web Audio oscillator and brownian noise synth.
